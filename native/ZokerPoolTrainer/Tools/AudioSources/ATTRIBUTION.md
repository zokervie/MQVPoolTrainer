# Recorded pool sound sources — 0.17

These are edited **HQ MP3 previews** of field recordings, not original lossless downloads. No Freesound API or account was used. Full source/license URLs, hashes and edits are in manifest.json.

Changes: selected time ranges, decoded/resampled/downmixed to 44.1 kHz mono PCM16, removed DC, applied short fades and peak gain. The cue sample selects the first recorded cue impact before the later ball collision. No synthetic recordings are labeled measured audio. Ball impacts have four recorded variants; cue, rail and pocket currently have one recording each with continuous event-dependent gain, not invented multi-layer recordings.
